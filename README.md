# Securing Digital Growth: Customer Data Protection and Privacy

**Case:** NetApp, "Securing Digital Business Growth" (NtapAT&T Digital), Problem 2

NtapAT&T Digital has 1 million customers and depends on trust. Our core solution is a Vendor Management Program that makes every outside company prove it protects customer data and follows privacy rules. It is backed by less access (least privilege, in-app 2FA, 15-minute auto-logout), less data kept (labeling, delete after 1 year of inactivity), and faster detection (logging and alerts). Each fix is tied to Finance, Sales, and Marketing value.
